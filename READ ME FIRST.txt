JOB TRACKER  -  free, offline job-search tracker for Mac
=========================================================

The full guide: double-click README.html in this folder (it opens in your web browser).
(README.md is the same guide; GitHub shows it on the project page.)

QUICK START
  1. Keep this whole folder together somewhere permanent, e.g. Documents/Job Tracker.
  2. First run of Setup: open Terminal, type  bash  and a space, drag Setup.command into the
     window, press Enter. After that, every .command file opens with a double-click.
  3. Open Job_Tracker.xlsx and fill in the yellow cells on the Settings, Areas and Watchlist tabs.
  4. Use it:
       Add Job        paste a job link -> the job is read, scored and saved
       Check Jobs     new postings at the companies on your Watchlist tab
       Check Email    application statuses from Gmail
       Rescore Jobs   re-rate saved jobs after changing your Settings

Updating: download the new version, double-click its Install Update, drag this folder in when asked.
Your tracker, logins and saved postings are never touched.
